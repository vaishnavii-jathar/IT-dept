About Our Department

Driving innovation and excellence in Information Technology.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality education in Information Technology, foster a research-oriented environment, and prepare ethically sound and technically competent professionals capable of addressing global challenges.

Our Vision

To be a leading center of excellence in Information Technology education and research, recognized for its innovative teaching methodologies, industry-relevant curriculum, and significant contributions to society.

A Legacy of IT Excellence

The Department of Information Technology at PDEA's College of Engineering, Manjari, has a proud history of academic rigor and practical innovation. Established with the aim of nurturing skilled IT professionals, our department has consistently evolved to meet the demands of the rapidly changing technological landscape.

We offer a dynamic curriculum that balances theoretical foundations with hands-on experience, ensuring our students are not just knowledgeable but also capable of applying their learning to real-world problems. Our faculty comprises experienced educators and industry experts who are committed to mentoring students and guiding them towards successful careers.

Beyond academics, we encourage holistic development through various co-curricular and extra-curricular activities, fostering teamwork, leadership, and a spirit of continuous learning.
