Welcome to the Department of Information Technology

The IT Department at PDEA's College of Engineering, Manjari, is a hub of innovation and learning. We are dedicated to providing a comprehensive education in cutting-edge technologies, fostering a strong coding culture, and preparing students for the dynamic challenges of the global IT industry. Our programs emphasize practical skills, research, and problem-solving, ensuring our graduates are well-equipped to drive technological advancements.

With state-of-the-art laboratories, experienced faculty, and a vibrant student community, we strive for excellence in every aspect of IT education. Join us to explore the endless possibilities in the world of information technology.
